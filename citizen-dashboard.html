AEGIS Emergency Flood Survival Guide LEVEL 4 SEVERE FLOOD WARNING
Official Evacuation Protocols & Survival Checklist • Assam State
CRITICAL EVACUATION PROTOCOLS
  • Move Immediately to Upper Grounds: If flood waters rise, head to upper floors or designated high ground shelters immediately.
  • Prepare Emergency Go-Bag: Pack drinking water (2L/person/day), non-perishable rations, flashlight, power bank, first-aid kit, and vital documents in waterproof plastic bags.
  • NEVER Walk or Drive in Flood Water: Just 6 inches of moving water can knock down an adult, and 2 feet of water can float and sweep away vehicles.
  • Follow Designated Safe Routes: Use the AEGIS Safe Routes map to avoid submerged roads like NH-715 near Bokakhat.
ELECTRICAL & GAS HAZARD PRECAUTIONS
  • Shut Off Main Power Breaker: Turn off the main electrical switch and LPG gas cylinder valve before evacuating.
  • Avoid Submerged Wiring: Never touch electrical switches or appliances if standing in water or wet surroundings.
  • Report Fallen Power Lines: Stay at least 30 feet away from downed electric poles and immediately report to Assam Power Distribution (APDCL: 1912).
WATER PURIFICATION & HEALTH ADVISORY
  • Boil Drinking Water: Boil all water vigorously for at least 1 minute or use Halazone/Chlorine water purification tablets.
  • Sanitize Wounds: Wash cuts or abrasions immediately with clean water and antiseptic to prevent waterborne infections (Leptospirosis, Cholera).
  • Beware of Wildlife & Snakes: Flooding forces snakes and reptiles to seek shelter in houses. Check shoes, bedding, and corners carefully.
