FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
How does the AI Safe Route Finder avoid flooded roads?
AEGIS continuously aggregates satellite water-level telemetry, citizen road block reports, and river basin gauge data. The system automatically computes detour waypoints around flooded roads to guide you safely to open relief camps.
What happens after I broadcast an SOS Distress Signal?
Your GPS coordinates, family headcount, and emergency type are instantly transmitted to the nearest NDRF/SDRF Battalion Command Center. A unique tracking ID is generated, allowing you to track the dispatched rescue boat live on the map.
Can I use this portal without active cellular internet?
Yes. Click "Download Offline Map Directions" on the Safe Routes page to cache street directions and emergency hotline contacts directly in your local device storage. You can also broadcast SOS via SMS to 51969.
How are relief shelters prioritized and allocated?
Shelters are categorized based on live capacity, medical facilities, ration availability, and elevation safety. The system dynamically points you to the closest operational facility with verified bed availability.
What should I do if a family member requires medical evacuation?
When dispatching an SOS, choose Medical Emergency as the primary category and check the Elderly / Infant / Patient present checkbox. SDRF paramedics with oxygen support and inflatable stretchers will be dispatched first.

ASSAM FLOOD SAFETY & EVACUATION PROTOCOL
Issued by Assam State Disaster Management Authority (ASDMA)

🚨 Immediate Life-Saving Instructions

  • Move to higher ground immediately: Do not wait until floodwater reaches your door.
  • Disconnect main power & gas: Turn off main electrical switches to prevent electrocution.
  • Avoid walking or driving through moving water: 6 inches of moving water can knock you down.
  • Keep emergency communication lines open: Save phone battery for critical rescue broadcasts.

🚶 Evacuation & Safe Transit Protocol

  • Follow official designated safe routes shown in the Safe Routes portal.
  • Do not cross breached river embankments (Bunds) or submerged bridges.
  • Signal rescue boats with brightly colored cloth, torches, or sound whistles.
  • Give priority to infants, pregnant women, elderly citizens, and individuals with disabilities.

🎒 72-Hour Emergency Grab-and-Go Kit

  • Waterproof pouch with identification cards, property documents, and emergency cash.
  • Bottled drinking water (minimum 3 litres per person) and non-perishable high-energy rations.
  • Flashlight with spare batteries, power banks, and whistle for audio signaling.
  • Basic first-aid kit with antiseptic lotion, bandages, and prescribed medications for 7 days.

🩺 Water-Borne Disease Prevention & Hygiene

  • Boil all drinking water for at least 1 minute or use Halazone water purification tablets.
  • Do not consume food that has come into direct contact with flood waters.
  • Wash hands with soap or alcohol sanitizer before eating.
  • Seek immediate medical assistance at relief camps if showing symptoms of fever or diarrhea.
